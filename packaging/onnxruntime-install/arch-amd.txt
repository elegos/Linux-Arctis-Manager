Arch ships an official ROCm-accelerated build of ONNX Runtime:

    sudo pacman -S onnxruntime-rocm

This pulls in the ROCm stack (rocm-hip-sdk and friends) as a dependency. If you'd rather not, the CPU-only build also works, just without GPU acceleration:

    sudo pacman -S onnxruntime

After installing, click "Verify" below.
